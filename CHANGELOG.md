## [0.1.0] - Unreleased

### Added

- Initial project architecture and foundational domain types.
- Gregorian calendar adapter and proleptic math.
- Buddhist calendar adapter and Thai official exact presets.
- Strict ISO 8601 date and RFC 3339 instant parsers.
- Localized date, time, date-time, and range formatting with bounded LRU caching.
- Runtime capability inspection and timezone projection.
